# Canteen staff quick guide

## Before service

- Sign in using the account the admin created for your canteen.
- Open **Dashboard** and confirm the correct canteen is selected.
- Press **Enable sound** once if you want a beep for new orders.
- Check that menu prices and availability are current in **Manage Menu**.

## Preparing an order

1. A new order appears on the dashboard. Read its items, quantity, pickup time, and any note.
2. Press **Start preparing** when work begins.
3. Press **Mark ready** only when the order is ready at the counter.
4. The student shows the short-lived QR on their ready-order screen or gives you the four-digit pickup code.
5. To scan, press **Start QR scanner** and allow camera access. Camera scanning requires HTTPS and a supported browser; if it is unavailable or the QR cannot be used, enter the four-digit code on the order card.
6. Press **Collect** only after server verification and handing over the order. QR collection is single-use.
7. Confirm **Payment received** only after cash or UPI has actually been received at the counter. If payment is still due, leave it unchecked and use **Confirm payment** later.

There is no online payment or delivery. Never ask a student for their password, login token, or a QR screenshot through chat.

## No-show and menu changes

- Mark an order no-show only when it is still ready and the student did not collect it within the allowed time. One no-show blocks the student's account immediately.
- In **Blocked**, confirm the full amount of each no-show order only after receiving cash or UPI at the counter. Unblock is enabled only after all listed no-show orders are recorded as paid.
- Do not share student contact details outside the canteen team.
- In **Manage Menu**, update the price or mark an item sold out when needed. Changes apply to new orders; existing orders retain their saved price.
- Use **Blocked** to record no-show order payments and unblock students. There is no separate fine and no online payment.
- Use **Sales Report** for order totals; collected revenue is based on collected orders and is not an online payment ledger.

## If something goes wrong

- Check the selected canteen and refresh the page.
- If an order is missing, check the Dashboard again after a short wait; live updates have a polling fallback.
- Report the approximate time, order number, and steps to reproduce to the admin. Do not send passwords, tokens, or screenshots containing private information.
